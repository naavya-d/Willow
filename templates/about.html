About Willow

Willow is a web-based mental health chatbot designed to provide a calm and supportive space for users to express their thoughts and feelings.

The chatbot is built to listen without judgment and respond in a gentle and understanding way. It can help users reflect, feel heard, and take a moment to pause when things feel overwhelming.

Willow is not a replacement for professional mental health support. It is meant to act as an additional tool for emotional support and self-reflection.
